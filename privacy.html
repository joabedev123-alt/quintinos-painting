Privacy

This page explains how the Quintino's Painting LLC website handles information. The company is at 121 Bow St, Medford, MA 02155. Questions about this page can go to quintinospainting@gmail.com or (857) 268-3997.

Contact form

If you send the estimate form, you provide your name, phone number, email address, project type, property location, and message. That information is used to reply about the project. It is delivered to quintinospainting@gmail.com through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). FormSubmit processes the submission so it can reach the company inbox.

There is no newsletter on this site, and the form is not used to build a marketing list.

Calls, texts, and email

If you call, text, or email Flavio, that conversation is handled as a normal business message about your project.

What this site does not do

The site does not sell personal information. It does not run advertising cookies or an analytics tracker. Instagram and Google Maps links leave this site and follow the policies of those services.

Your request

If you want a message you sent through the form corrected or deleted, email quintinospainting@gmail.com. This page was last updated on November 17, 2026.
